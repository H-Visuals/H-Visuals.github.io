FAQ

The questions creators ask before we start.

How much does a video cost?+

It depends on length and how much motion graphics work the edit needs. Most long-form videos land in a fixed per-video rate we agree before I start, so there are no surprises. Monthly retainers for weekly uploads get a lower rate.

How long does an edit take?+

Seven days from footage to final cut, including revisions. Shorts and clips usually go out within 48 hours.

How many revisions do I get?+

Two rounds are included. In practice most videos are approved on the first cut because the story angle is agreed before I open the timeline.

What do you need from me?+

Raw footage, your brand assets if you have them, and a sentence on who the video is for. I handle the rest, including music licensing and captions.

Why you over a cheaper editor?+

I edit for the outcome, not the runtime. Channels I've worked on reach a combined audience of over 3 million, and the edits are built around retention and conversion rather than effects for their own sake.

How do we start?+

Send a message with a link to your channel. I reply within 24 hours with a short plan and a quote for the first video.
